### Item 4: Scrum Overview
- **Introduced by:** Audria (TA)
- **Discussion:** The development process should be centered around user stories to make sure the features being prioritized fits the users' need. A backlog of tasks and features will be created based on the user stories, and each sprint will focus on a subset of the backlog. Each sprint consists of a planning meeting at the start, stand-up meetings throughout, and a review and retrospective at the ned. The sprint review focuses on the product, code, features, and backlog, while the sprint retrospective focuses on the team dynamics. Audria emphasized the importance of meeting with shareholders for clarification and giving them expectations on the end product. This can include having stakeholders join the sprint review to see a demo of the product and be aware of development processes and decisions. A Scrum Master other than team leads may be assigned to supervise the scrum process.
- **Resolution / Status:** Resolved — Scrum process explained.

### Item 5: Risk Assessment
- **Introduced by:** Audria (TA)
- **Discussion:** When considering a cool but time-consuming feature, a risk assessment should be made on how many people and how much time will be allocated for that feature. There is also a people risk to consider on whether the task can be completed given the team dynamic. Architectural decisions should be in an ADR (Architectural Design Report), which includes the decision and justification for the decision. The ADR is important for minimizing the bus factor.
- **Resolution / Status:** Resolved — risk assessment explained.

### Item 6: Prototype And Data Collection Clarification
- **Introduced by:** Evan Marriott
- **Discussion:**  Evan asked for clarifications about what a prototype should look like in the first sprint and also how data will be collected for the WatchTower dashboard. The main focus of the first sprint is figuring out the UI design for the dashboard. The most important features for the MVP are error flagging, user feedback, and performance metrics. For the data, a JavaScript file will be injected into the target software. The JavaScript will collect data from the target software and send it to an endpoint, where it would be saved in the database. There can then be visualizations of the data in the form of graphs in the dashboard.
- **Resolution / Status:** Resolved — prototype and data collection process clarified.
