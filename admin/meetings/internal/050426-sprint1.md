## Decisions Made

### Decision 1: Sprint 1 Will Be One Week Long
- **Decision:** Sprint 1 will last one week and end at the next Monday meeting.
- **Reasoning:** Sprint 1 is mostly focused on research, planning, defining the MVP, and creating a prototype, so a shorter sprint makes sense. A one-week sprint also allows the team to regroup quickly and adjust before more complex development work begins.
- **Dissenting Opinions:** No major dissenting opinions were raised.
- **Why the group proceeded anyway:** The team agreed that a shorter first sprint would keep everyone aligned and make it easier to review progress.

### Decision 2: Stand-Ups Will Be Done Asynchronously in Slack
- **Decision:** Stand-ups will be completed through Slack rather than through live meetings.
- **Reasoning:** The group only needs brief updates for stand-ups, so asynchronous Slack updates are more efficient and easier for everyone to complete.
- **Dissenting Opinions:** No major dissenting opinions were raised.
- **Why the group proceeded anyway:** The team agreed that Slack updates satisfy the stand-up requirement while avoiding unnecessary meetings.

### Decision 3: Roles Will Be Assigned for Sprint 1
- **Decision:** Roles will be assigned for Sprint 1, and team members will work on tasks connected to those roles.
- **Reasoning:** Assigning roles helps the team divide work clearly and makes it easier to organize tasks in the task tracker.
- **Dissenting Opinions:** No major dissenting opinions were raised.
- **Why the group proceeded anyway:** The group preferred random or assigned roles for this sprint and may rotate roles in future weeks.

### Decision 4: The Team Will Use Figma for Product and Design Work
- **Decision:** Figma will be used as a shared workspace for prototype, research, user flows, personas, and visual planning.
- **Reasoning:** Figma provides a shared place to organize design work and makes it easier for the product/design team to collaborate.
- **Dissenting Opinions:** Some members were not familiar with Figma, but no major objections were raised.
- **Why the group proceeded anyway:** Figma is relatively easy to learn and useful for organizing early design work.

### Decision 5: Sprint 1 Retrospective Will Be Held During the Next Monday Meeting
- **Decision:** The Sprint 1 retrospective will likely take place at the beginning of next Monday's meeting.
- **Reasoning:** This avoids needing to schedule an additional meeting and uses existing meeting time efficiently.
- **Dissenting Opinions:** No major dissenting opinions were raised.
- **Why the group proceeded anyway:** The team agreed that the Monday meeting should have enough time for a short retrospective.
